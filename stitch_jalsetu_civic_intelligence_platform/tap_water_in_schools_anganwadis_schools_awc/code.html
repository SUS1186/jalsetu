Special 100-Day Campaign Trajectory

Oct 2020 – Present
Campaign Kickoff (Gandhi Jayanti 2020) Oct 2, 2020

Shri Gajendra Singh Shekhawat, Hon'ble Minister for Jal Shakti, launched the dedicated 100-day drive to ensure clean potable running water in all schools and child-care institutions.

Poshan Abhiyaan Convergence Aug 2022

Integrated with Poshan 2.0 to mandate piped water for all midday hot-cooked meals and mandatory provision of low-height child taps in all Anganwadi learning centres.

Bi-Annual NABL Water Testing Mandate Active Cycle 2024

State PHE laboratories instructed to test school tap water samples across 13 basic parameters free of cost, uploading records directly to WQMIS portal.

Protocol C1

Child-Centric Infrastructure

Standard operating procedure mandates taps located at 45 cm to 60 cm height from floor level in Anganwadis and Primary schools, ensuring easy self-access by toddlers and young learners without risk of slips.

  • check_circle Push-type self-closing water-saver taps
  • check_circle Non-slip masonry ramps with grab rails for CWSN children
  • check_circle Rounded edge washbasins with soap dispensers

Protocol C2

Bi-Annual Potability Testing

School drinking water taps are tested twice a year: pre-monsoon (April-May) and post-monsoon (October-November) across 13 chemical & bacteriological parameters in certified PHE labs.

  • check_circle Zero testing fee for all Govt schools and AWCs
  • check_circle Direct electronic upload to WQMIS portal
  • check_circle Immediate SMS alert to Headmaster if coliform detected
WQMIS Protocol BIS 10500:2012

Protocol C3

Greywater to Poshan Vatika

Wash water collected from handwash basins and vegetable rinsing in mid-day meal kitchens is naturally filtered through a gravel-and-reed bed and channeled directly into school vegetable gardens.

  • check_circle Fresh leafy vegetables for PM-POSHAN mid-day meals
  • check_circle Prevents water logging and mosquito vector breeding
  • check_circle Managed actively by student Eco-Club ambassadors

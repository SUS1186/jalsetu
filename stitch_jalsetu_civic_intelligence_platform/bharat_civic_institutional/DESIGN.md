## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, highly accessible, and dense public governance framework for national digital infrastructure. It addresses citizens, district collectors, field engineers, and water quality analysts across India. The aesthetic communicates constitutional stability, data verifiability, and administrative transparency.

The overarching design aesthetic is **Corporate / Modern Institutional**, infused with functional data density. It avoids frivolous ornamentation, excessive border radiuses, and ambient shadows in favor of precise structural boundaries, crisp 1px borders, high-contrast typography, and purposeful data encoding. Clear color coding guides public accountability across administrative tiers from the central secretariat down to the Gram Panchayat.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for institutional rigor, high legibility under varying display conditions, and categorical clarity:

- **Bharat Navy (`#002147`)**: Represents sovereign authority and national infrastructure. Applied to primary headers, top-tier administrative banners, and high-emphasis interface chrome.
- **NIC Royal Blue (`#0B5CAB`)**: The operational workhorse for primary actions, navigational highlights, interactive state definitions, and data visualization emphasis.
- **Ashoka Emblem Gold (`#B3802A`)**: Reserved strictly for sovereign seals, official certifications, verified water quality accolades, and apex indicators.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F4F6F9`) & Surface White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Establishes a calm, cool-tinted backdrop that reduces eye strain during prolonged inspection of dense statistical tables.
- **Administrative Tiers**: Four functional pastel fills delineate administrative hierarchy at a glance:
  - Districts: Soft Lavender (`#E8EEFA`)
  - Blocks: Pale Peach (`#FDEAEB`)
  - Panchayats: Light Periwinkle (`#EBF3FC`)
  - Villages: Pale Mint (`#E8F8F0`)
- **6-Tier Choropleth Map Scale**: Standardized linear progression for progress visualization (`#F7D9CE` through `#08306B`), ensuring accessibility across choropleth maps and categorical data grids.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes uncompromised legibility for civic engagement:

- **Public Sans** serves as the primary typeface for all interfaces, headings, and editorial content. Designed specifically for government portals, it offers high neutral legibility, robust optical weights, and optimal character distinction.
- **JetBrains Mono** is utilized strictly for tabular data, percentages, counts, timestamp records, and water quality metrics (pH, TDS, Turbidity). Monospaced alignment guarantees zero jitter during continuous live metric sorting.
- **Numeral Formatting System**: Numerical output in data tables must follow the Indian numbering system using standard lac and crore separation format (e.g., `12,34,56,789`), rendered in tabular tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected around an administrative 12-column grid system tuned for high information throughput:

- **Grid Strategy**: Fluid 12-column grid bounded within a maximum content container of 1440px on desktop screens, collapsing into a 6-column grid on tablets (768px - 1024px) and 4 columns on mobile viewports (<768px).
- **Rhythm**: Compact 4px base modular unit. Civic monitoring dashboards demand vertical space conservation to fit comprehensive state/district tables within the initial viewport.
- **Dense Data Containers**: Table rows standardize to a compact 36px height with 8px horizontal cell padding. Summary cards adhere to strict `space-md` internal padding with 1px outer structure.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, floating neo-morphism or exaggerated drop shadows, opting instead for **Low-contrast Structural Outlines** and crisp 1px borders:

- **Layering Principle**: Flat elevation achieved through distinct surface contrast (`#F4F6F9` canvas vs. `#FFFFFF` cards) bordered with `#DCE3EC`.
- **Modals & Overlays**: Elevation 1 is strictly restricted to interactive dialogs, multi-select administrative filters, and map hover tooltips. It utilizes a razor-sharp, low-spread drop shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(0, 33, 71, 0.08)` paired with a `1px solid #B0C0D4` outline.
- **Interactive State**: Hover states on interactive cards or data rows do not lift vertically; they transition background luminosity to an active neutral tint or apply a solid 2px outline in `#0B5CAB`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft (`1`)** shape language to preserve institutional credibility:

- Standard base radius of `0.25rem` (4px) applied uniformly to buttons, text inputs, status chips, and table containers.
- Cards, administrative modules, and panel containers utilize `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Circles are reserved exclusively for status indicator dots, verification badges, and pagination counters. Pill-shaped components are strictly prohibited to maintain professional dignity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Bharat Navy (`#002147`) background, text in `#FFFFFF`, 4px radius, 36px total height, horizontal padding of `1rem`. Active states transition to `#0B5CAB`.
- **Secondary**: 1px border in `#0B5CAB`, transparent background, text in `#0B5CAB`. Hover background transitions to `#EBF3FC`.
- **Export Action (Civic Specific)**: Distinct functional variant with 1px border in `#DCE3EC`, white background, neutral text, incorporating standardized PDF/Excel micro-icons.

### Administrative Tier Cards
- Structured metric cards representing the 4-tier hierarchy (Districts, Blocks, Panchayats, Villages).
- Background colors strictly adhere to their respective tokens (`card-district`, `card-block`, `card-panchayat`, `card-village`).
- Top edge highlighted by a 3px accent band in `#0B5CAB`. Metrics rendered via `label-numeric-lg` with lac/crore delineation.

### Dense Data Tables
- Header: Fixed `#002147` background with uppercase bold `#FFFFFF` text, `label-ui` style, right-aligned for numeric metrics, left-aligned for administrative names.
- Body Rows: Alternating zebra striping (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`), bordered with 1px `#DCE3EC`. 
- Numeric cells utilize JetBrains Mono with Indian commas.

### Chips & Verification Status
- Compact 20px height, 4px corner radius.
- **Certified (Har Ghar Jal Verified)**: Solid `#E8F8F0` background, `#1A7F48` border and text, preceded by a checkmark badge.
- **Contaminated / Critical**: Solid `#FDEAEB` background, `#C82333` border and text.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#DCE3EC` border, 36px height.
- Focus state: Replaces border with a solid 2px `#0B5CAB` outline; no glowing blur effects. Administrative dropdown filters must support quick typeahead searching for bilingual region names.
